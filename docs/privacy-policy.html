Khushory — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 · Contact: techfre.e46@gmail.com

What Khushory stores

Khushory keeps the laundry-shop records you enter: customer names and phone numbers, drop-off slips, garment items and services, payments, balances and expenses.

Where the data lives

All records are stored only on the computer running Khushory, in a locally encrypted (SQLCipher) database whose key is protected by the operating system's credential store. The app makes no internet requests — nothing is uploaded to us or to any third party, and there are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

Who controls the data

The shop is the data controller. The publisher of Khushory has no access to your records. The shop is responsible for access control (user PINs and roles are provided in the app) and for backups.

Optional LAN sharing

A "share on WiFi" mode lets another computer on the same local network open the app in a browser. It is off by default, requires a randomly generated access code, and never exposes data beyond the local network. No cloud relay is used.

Backups

Manual and scheduled backups are written only to folders you choose (e.g. a USB drive). Their handling is the shop's responsibility.

Changes

This policy may be updated with app releases; the latest version ships inside the app and on this page.
